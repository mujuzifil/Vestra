## Enhancements

  - Tags
  - Addresses
  - Orders
  - Payments
  - Activity timeline

- Bulk actions: Assign Tag, Remove Tag.
- `CustomerTagResource` for managing customer segments.
- `CustomerNote` model and enum support general, support, sales, and internal note types.
