# Stage 18.9 — Executive Dashboard Report

## Objective
Provide administrators with a unified executive view of the VESTRA business.

## Widgets delivered
- **ExecutiveKpiWidget**: Today's, weekly, and monthly revenue with trend indicators; orders today.
- **OperationalKpiWidget**: Pending orders, awaiting payment, low stock, new messages, reviews to moderate.
- **InventoryValueWidget**: Total inventory value, low stock items, out-of-stock items, active warehouses.
- **OutstandingCreditWidget**: Outstanding credit, pending credit approvals, pending payment uploads, pending distributor applications.
- **RevenueChartWidget** and **OrderStatusChartWidget**: Visual revenue and order-status trends.
- **RecentOrdersWidget**, **TopDistributorsWidget**, **LowStockWidget**, **AlertsWidget**, **RecentActivityWidget**.

## Dashboard registration
All widgets are registered in `App\Filament\Pages\Dashboard::getWidgets()`.

## Status
✅ Dashboard expanded with inventory, credit, and distributor KPIs.
