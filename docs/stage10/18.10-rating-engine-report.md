# Stage 18.10 — Rating Engine Report

## Overview
The rating engine aggregates customer feedback into actionable product metrics.

## Capabilities
- Average rating computed per product from approved, visible reviews.
- Rating distribution returned with every product review listing (5★ through 1★).
- Rating colors indicate sentiment (success/warning/danger).
- Review statistics displayed on product detail pages and in admin review tables.

## Future Enhancements
- Category- and brand-level rating averages.
- Rating trend charts over time.
