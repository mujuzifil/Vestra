# Stage 18.11 — Business Intelligence Report

## Overview

Stage 18.11 extends the existing `ReportService` with comprehensive Business Intelligence capabilities covering revenue, sales, customer, distributor, inventory, engagement, search, and operational domains.

## New ReportService Methods

### Executive Summary
- `executiveSummary($start, $end)` — consolidated KPIs across customers, inventory, engagement, distributors, and finance.

### Customer Intelligence
- `customerSegments()` — RFM-style segments (VIP, Loyal, New, At Risk, Lost).
- `customerLifetimeValue($limit)` — top customers by lifetime spend.
- `customerRetentionRate($start, $end)` — period-over-period retention.
- `customerChurnRate($days)` — customers who stopped purchasing.
- `topCustomerRegions($limit)` — geographic revenue distribution.
- `customerActivityTimeline($limit)` — recent orders and registrations.

### Distributor Intelligence
- `distributorRevenue($start, $end)` — revenue by distributor.
- `distributorOrders($start, $end)` — daily distributor order trends.
- `distributorCreditUtilization()` — credit limits and utilization.
- `distributorOutstandingBalances()` — unpaid distributor invoices.
- `distributorPerformanceTrend($start, $end)` — combined orders and applications trend.

### Inventory Intelligence
- `inventoryTurnover($start, $end)` — sold vs stock turnover ratio.
- `stockValuationByCategory()` — stock value by category.
- `warehouseUtilization()` — quantity, reserved, and available by warehouse.
- `deadStock($days, $limit)` — products with no recent sales.

### Search & Engagement Intelligence
- `searchConversionMetrics($days)` — searches, zero-result rate, CTR, conversions.
- `reviewAnalytics($days)` — review volume, ratings, helpful votes, reports.
- `wishlistAnalytics()` — wishlist totals and top products.
- `recommendationEffectiveness($days)` — baseline for recommendation attribution.

### Operational Monitoring
- `queueHealth()` — failed and pending jobs.
- `recentFailedJobs($limit)` — recent failed job details.
- `schedulerStatus()` — last scheduler run.
- `storageStatus()` — disk usage.
- `cacheStatus()` — cache driver and reachability.
- `notificationDeliveryMetrics($days)` — notification read rate.

## Caching

All new methods use the existing 1-hour cache TTL and deterministic cache keys to protect production performance.
