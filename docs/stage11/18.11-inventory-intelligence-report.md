# Stage 18.11 — Inventory Intelligence Report

## Overview

Inventory intelligence provides turnover analysis, category valuation, warehouse utilization, and dead-stock identification.

## Metrics

- Inventory turnover ratio by product
- Stock valuation grouped by category
- Warehouse quantity, reserved, and available stock
- Dead stock — products with no sales in 90+ days
- Fast and slow moving products

## Filament Page

`InventoryIntelligenceReport` shows valuation doughnut chart, turnover table, warehouse utilization, and dead-stock table.

## API

- `GET /api/v1/reports/inventory`
- `GET /api/v1/reports/inventory-intelligence`
