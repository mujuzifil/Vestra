# Phase 6 — Request a Quote Experience Design Report

## Objective
Transform `/request-quote` into the flagship commercial enquiry page for VESTRA®.

## Sections Delivered

1. **Hero**
   - Gradient header with commercial-enquiry badge.
   - Headline: "Get a Custom Quote for Your Business".
   - Supporting paragraph and Request My Quote / Contact Sales CTAs.

2. **Who Can Request a Quote**
   - Twelve cards covering hotels, hospitals, schools, commercial laundries, cleaning companies, manufacturers, NGOs, government, supermarkets, wholesale businesses, corporate offices, and industrial facilities.

3. **Why Request a Quote**
   - Six value cards: Bulk Pricing, Manufacturer Direct, Flexible Quantities, Professional Advice, Reliable Supply, Fast Response.

4. **Quote Process**
   - Six-step timeline: Submit Request → Sales Review → Requirements Discussion → Quotation Preparation → Customer Approval → Order Processing.

5. **Quote Request Form**
   - Grouped sections: Customer Information, Business Information, Product Requirements, Delivery Requirements, Additional Notes, Attachments.
   - Repeatable product rows with product select, package size, quantity, and notes.
   - Multi-file upload for POs, requirement documents, and images.
   - Inline success state with reference number and navigation buttons.

6. **Customer Support**
   - Phone, WhatsApp, email, and business-hours cards.

7. **FAQ**
   - Seven common questions about MOQ, lead time, delivery, payment, packaging, multiple products, and support.

8. **Related Resources**
   - Cards linking Products, Become a Distributor, Where to Buy, and Blog.

9. **Final CTA**
   - Dark gradient section with Call Sales, WhatsApp, and Email buttons.

## Design System Compliance
- Reuses existing gradient hero, `ValueCard`, `SectionHeader`, `FAQAccordion`, `AnimatedItem`, and `Container`.
- Responsive grids adapt from 1 to 2–4 columns.
- No e-commerce terminology or shopping actions remain.
