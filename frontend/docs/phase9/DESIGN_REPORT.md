# Phase 9 — Corporate Contact Experience Design Report

## Objective

Transform `/contact` from a basic enquiry page into a premium corporate communication hub that gives customers, distributors, institutions, and partners every legitimate way to reach VESTRA®.

## Sections Delivered

1. **Hero**
   - Gradient header with "We're Here to Help" headline.
   - Supporting copy focused on products, distribution, and advice.
   - CTAs: Contact Sales (phone), Request a Quote.

2. **Get in Touch**
   - Four premium contact cards: Sales, Email, Business Hours, Office.
   - Sales card includes phone and WhatsApp.
   - Email card includes general and sales addresses.

3. **Send us a Message + Google Maps**
   - Professional enquiry form with company, phone, enquiry type, and attachments.
   - Live Google Maps embed with directions link to the official location.

4. **Join the VESTRA® Community**
   - Social cards for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, and WhatsApp Channel.
   - Each card includes icon, description, and visit link.

5. **FAQ**
   - Six contact-focused questions.

6. **Related Resources**
   - Products, Request a Quote, Become a Distributor, Knowledge Centre, Where to Buy.

7. **Final CTA**
   - Call Sales, WhatsApp, Email.

## Design System Compliance

- Reuses `Container`, `SectionHeader`, `ContactCard`, `FAQAccordion`, `AnimatedSection`, and `Icon`.
- Consistent gradient heroes, rounded cards, and B2B language.
- No e-commerce functionality or placeholder content.
