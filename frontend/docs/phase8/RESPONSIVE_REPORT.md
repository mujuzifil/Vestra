# Phase 8 — Responsive Report

## Breakpoints

- Mobile: single-column layouts, stacked CTAs, full-width cards.
- Tablet: two-column article grid and category grid.
- Desktop: three-column article grid, four-column category grid, side-by-side featured article.

## Verified Layouts

- Hero text scales with `clamp()`.
- Search/filter bar stacks on mobile and aligns horizontally on desktop.
- Article cards maintain consistent aspect ratios.
- Featured article image sits above text on mobile and beside it on desktop.
- Newsletter form fields stack on small screens.
